Alerts

The panel emails you if the signal is lost for 10 minutes, a process has to be restarted, the disk is nearly full, the Pi overheats, or the National Weather Service issues a warning or watch from the New York (OKX) office. If goesrecv or goesproc dies or hangs, the watchdog restarts it.

Phone push notifications

    
Alerts (signal lost, restarts, weather warnings, skipped briefing) can also be pushed to your phone with the free ntfy app. Install ntfy, subscribe to a topic name that is hard to guess, and enter the same name here. Anyone who knows the topic could read the alerts, so make it long and random. Alert text passes through ntfy.sh.
